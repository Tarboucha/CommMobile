## Notes for Legal Review

Before launch, a lawyer should specifically verify:

1. **Impressum completeness** — Exact requirements depend on your Rechtsform (GbR, UG, GmbH, etc.)
2. **Marketplace liability model** — Whether KoDo could be classified as a „marketplace operator" with extended duties under the new EU marketplace regulations
3. **Kleinunternehmerregelung** — If applicable, affects what you display regarding VAT
4. **Verpackungsgesetz** — If physical goods are shipped, packaging law obligations
5. **Lebensmittelhygiene** — If food items are offered through the platform
6. **Handwerksordnung** — If handcraft services are offered, licensing requirements
7. **DAC7** — Platform reporting obligations to tax authorities starting 2023
8. **Digital Services Act (DSA)** — Content moderation transparency requirements
9. **Jugendschutz** — Age verification requirements if applicable
10. **Versicherung** — Professional liability and platform liability insurance needs
